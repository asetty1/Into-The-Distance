# Into The Distance


## 🏇 Overview
**Into The Distance** is an  **endless runner** where you, the player, ride a horse through a landscape. Your goal? **Stay on the path, dodge obstacles, and rack up the highest score possible!**

## 🎮 Gameplay
- Control your **horse**.
- Avoid **trees** that could hurt or slow down your horse.
- Travel as far as possible to achieve **the highest score**!

## 📜 Scoring
- Your score is based on the **distance traveled**.
- Be careful, you lose points for every tree **you hit**!
- The longer you survive, the higher your score climbs!
